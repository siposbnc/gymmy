The mechanism

Timestamp-driven, not interval-driven

  • Not this: a setInterval counting down. It dies the moment the phone sleeps or the browser freezes the tab, and you come back to a timer that stopped two minutes ago.
  • Gymmy stores the timer's absolute end timestamp.
  • Remaining time is derived on every render and on visibilitychange — so returning to the app shows the truth, however long you were away.
  • A setInterval only drives the visual tick. If it stalls, nothing is lost.
  • Completion fires from a notification scheduled against the timestamp, not from JavaScript staying alive.

Platform fallbacks

Three independent signals

  • Notification — needs an installed PWA on iOS 16.4+. Reliable once Gymmy ships as a native build via Capacitor.
  • Vibration — Android only. Safari has no Vibration API at all.
  • In-app — sound plus the state change above. Always works, and it's why the timer never depends on the other two.

The rail doubles as the recovery indicator. Reopen the app after ninety seconds away and the rail is already in the right place — no spinner, no resync.
