Why a custom pad

54px keys against the OS keyboard's ~30px number row. It carries ±2.5 increments alongside the digits, keeps a decimal point for half plates, and never covers the thing being edited. It also means the same interaction on desktop, where a numeric row would otherwise be fine but inconsistent.
